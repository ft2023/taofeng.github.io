Research Focus

Agentic Reinforcement Learning & LLM Reasoning

I develop reinforcement learning methods for multi-round model routing and aggregation, evolving rubric-based rewards for audio reasoning, and self-generated training signals through multi-agent co-evolution. This work extends to curiosity-driven test-time learning, time-series reasoning, and graph-based world models.

Experience Learning & Self-Improving Agents

I study how agents encode and retrieve reusable experience through latent representations, a trainable experience harness with graph-based retrieval, and retrieved reasoning traces. I also develop learnable memory allocation, evolving memory skills, cost-aware memory budgets, and memory-based planning and personalization.

Agentic Planning, Routing & Evaluation

I build graph-based LLM routing and unified routing infrastructure, including xRouteBench. I study interactive knowledge extraction, long-context evaluation, research-idea evaluation, and scientific agent environments, and use multi-LLM logs to learn complementary model capabilities.

Reinforcement Learning & Sequential Decision Making

My earlier research applied reinforcement and imitation learning to delivery routing, epidemic mobility interventions, and protein complex assembly. I also studied large-scale multi-agent coordination using graph attention and mean-field approximations.
